Which Walking Dead Character Are You?

The Walking Dead Personality Quiz is a fictional personality test based on difficult decisions inspired by the world of The Walking Dead.

Instead of simply asking which character you like, the quiz analyzes how you approach survival, loyalty, morality, leadership, trust and risk.

Your answers are scored across 16 hidden personality traits and compared against a character matrix to find your closest matches.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Walking Dead personality quiz work?

You face 30 fictional situations and choose the response that best represents what you would do. Your choices are analyzed across 16 hidden personality traits.

How many questions are in the quiz?

The quiz contains 30 questions, with five choices for each question.

How is my Walking Dead character determined?

Your personality profile is compared against the character profiles in the quiz's character matrix. The three closest matches are shown on the results page.

Can I get a different character on another attempt?

Yes. Try the quiz again and make different decisions to see how your personality profile and character matches change.

Is this a scientific personality test?

No. This is a fictional entertainment experience and is not a psychological or scientific assessment.
